Print sizes look like a random list of numbers: 4×6, 5×7, 8×10, 8×12, 12×18. They aren't random, and knowing why saves you from a print with someone's head cut off at the edge. Sizes here are in inches, as they're sold in Nepal.

## Your photo has a shape

Every photo has an aspect ratio: its width compared to its height.

- **Camera photos are 3:2.** DSLR and mirrorless cameras make photos in this shape.
- **Phone photos are usually 4:3.** A little squarer than a camera photo.

A print whose shape matches the photo shows all of it. A print with a different shape has to cut something off the edges.

| Size (in) | Shape | Fits a camera photo? | Fits a phone photo? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4×6 | 3:2 | Yes, whole | Crops a little |
| 5×7 | 7:5 | Crops a little | Crops a little |
| 6×8 | 4:3 | Crops a little | Yes, whole |
| 8×10 | 5:4 | Crops more | Crops a little |
| 8×12 | 3:2 | Yes, whole | Crops a little |
| 12×18 | 3:2 | Yes, whole | Crops a little |

A little cropping is often fine, but in a tight family group the person at each end is the one who loses out. Tell us who must stay in the picture and we'll check before printing.

## How big can a photo be printed?

A sharp print needs enough pixels. A useful rule: aim for about 200 to 300 pixels for every inch of print. Larger prints hung on a wall are seen from further away, so they can get by with less.

- A **4×6** print needs about 1200 × 1800 pixels for full quality.
- A modern phone photo (12 megapixels, 4032 × 3024) prints well up to about **12×16**.
- For **16×24 and larger**, use the original from a camera if you can.

The catch is how the photo reaches us. **WhatsApp shrinks photos** sent the normal way, and Facebook and Instagram do the same. Send the photo as a document on WhatsApp, or bring the original on a pen drive, and it arrives at full size.

## Which size for which place

| Where it goes | Size to consider |
| --- | --- |
| A table, a shelf, a gift | 5×7 or 6×8 |
| A desk or a small wall | 8×10 or 8×12 |
| A bedroom or hallway wall | 12×18 |
| Above a sofa or a bed | 16×24 |
| The main wall of a living room | 20×30 |

A useful test: cut a sheet of newspaper to the size and tape it to the wall for a day. Most people find they wanted one size bigger.

## Framed print or canvas?

A framed print behind glass keeps fine detail sharp, which matters in a big family group where every face should be recognisable. A canvas has no glass, so there's no glare from windows or lights, and its texture suits a single bold portrait. ## Old photos

Old family prints can be copied and printed again, but a small, faded print won't become sharp when it's enlarged. For a portrait of a late family member, bring the best original you have and we'll suggest the size it will hold up at.
